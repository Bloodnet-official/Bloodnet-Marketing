Modernizing Sri Lanka's Emergency Blood Logistics

Moving healthcare providers from manual phone trees to automated instant dispatch.

Traditional Manual Paper Lists

  • Hospital staff manually dial phone numbers from physical books during midnight emergencies.
  • Frequent calls made to donors who are ineligible (within the mandatory 90-day cooldown period).
  • No distance targeting—calling volunteers who live 30+ km away and cannot travel in time.
  • Panic-driven social media posts leading to unverified messaging and donor fatigue.

The BloodNet Digital Platform

  • Instant 1-Click Alerting: Triggers SMS and mobile push messages in less than 3 seconds.
  • 5–10km Geofencing: Mobilizes verified donors who can physically reach the hospital fast.
  • Automated Eligibility Engine: Smart 90-day cooldown filter ensures zero spam to ineligible donors.
  • Unified NBTS & Private Compliance: Streamlined inventory logs for regional blood banks.
Core Capabilities

Engineered for Critical Healthcare Needs

Key functionality designed to bridge blood banks, private clinics, and local communities.

1-Click Emergency Trigger

Lab staff select blood type and units needed to launch an emergency broadcast with a single click.

Real-Time Inventory Alerts

Automated indicators highlight low stock levels for rare blood groups (O-, AB-, A-) before severe shortages occur.

Instant SMS Gateway

Integration with Sri Lankan telco SMS gateways (Dialog/Mobitel) ensures messages reach donors even without internet access.

5–10km Geofenced Precision

Targets donors within immediate proximity to the hospital, reducing travel time during life-threatening procedures.

Automated 90-Day Cooldown

Smart rules prevent contacting donors who donated within the past 90 days, safeguarding volunteer health.

Dual Healthcare Support

Tailored dashboard workflows for National Blood Transfusion Service centers and accredited private hospitals.
